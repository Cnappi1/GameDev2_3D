How To Play:
Start By Opening ArriveHome_Outside Scene and Pressing Play
Anne is polite. She will not move while certain dialog and conversations are on the screen.
Be sure to press the E key to continue through dialog.
Anne moves using WASD Controls.
She also interacts with certain items in and outside the house by pressing the E key. 
Walking away from these items should get any pops up that display to go away.
Also, sometimes certain items will trigger dialog and internal thoughts for Anne. Remember to proceed through these using the E button.
Have fun and make sure to choose your path wisely...
